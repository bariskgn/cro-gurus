Our story

Started in Zürich, drawn from Japan

Ryūma began with a sketchbook and a simple frustration: streetwear with a good graphic rarely had a good garment underneath it. We design each artwork ourselves, choose heavier fabric than the price suggests, and explain both. Japanese influence sits in the drawing and the restraint, not in borrowed symbols.
