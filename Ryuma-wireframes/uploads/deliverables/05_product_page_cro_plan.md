# Ryūma Stage 1: Product Page CRO Plan

**Goal:** Prove design value, product quality and fit before asking the customer to purchase.

## Recommended section order

| Order | Section | What it contains |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Breadcrumbs | Home / Collection / Product. |
| 2 | Product gallery | Front, back, on-model, artwork detail, fabric/construction and scale. Support swipe, thumbnails and zoom. |
| 3 | Product summary | Product name, price, short design meaning and verified badge such as New or Limited. |
| 4 | Color selection | Labeled swatches, selected color name and immediate image update. |
| 5 | Size and fit | Size buttons, Size Guide, fit description and model height/worn size. |
| 6 | Purchase block | Quantity, primary Add to Cart button, stock state and optional accelerated payment. |
| 7 | Purchase reassurance | Delivery estimate/coverage, returns window and secure/local payment message. |
| 8 | Product evidence | Material, fabric weight, construction, decoration method, production origin and care. |
| 9 | Design story | Concise story behind the artwork with one supporting image. |
| 10 | Reviews/community | Verified reviews, fit feedback and customer imagery once genuine content exists. |
| 11 | Related products | Two to four complementary or same-design products. |

On desktop, keep the purchase block beside the gallery. On mobile, use a sticky Add to Cart bar after the main button leaves view.

## Above-the-fold purchase block

Include:

- Product name and active-market price
- One-sentence design/value statement
- Color and size selectors
- Clear unavailable combinations
- Size Guide link beside size selection
- Full-width Add to Cart
- Short shipping and returns reassurance

Disable Add to Cart until a size is selected and explain what is missing. Never preselect a size.

## Product gallery

- Use real product photography, not only mockups.
- Keep image ratios consistent.
- Show the garment on different body angles.
- Include close-ups that prove print, embroidery, fabric and stitching.
- Provide separate mobile crops where needed.
- Do not autoplay video.

## Size and fit

The Size Guide should open without losing the selected variant. Include:

- Garment measurements in cm
- How to measure
- Fit description: regular, relaxed or oversized
- Model height and worn size
- Simple guidance between sizes

Fit information must be product-specific when silhouettes differ.

## Purchase options

- Primary: Add to Cart
- Secondary: locally relevant accelerated checkout when supported
- Show accepted payment methods near the purchase area or cart
- Keep guest checkout available
- Do not let express-payment buttons compete visually with Add to Cart

## Content presentation

Use short accordions for Product Details, Size & Fit, Shipping & Returns and Care. Keep the most important fit and delivery facts visible outside the accordions.

The design story should support the product, not push the purchase controls below long editorial content.

## Visual direction

- Editorial gallery with a calm, neutral purchase panel
- Strong hierarchy for name, price, selectors and CTA
- Minimal icons and borders
- Brand display type for headings; readable sans-serif for commerce details
- One controlled accent color for selection and CTA states

## CRO rules

- Use specific proof instead of “premium quality.”
- Show total price, availability and delivery conditions clearly.
- Avoid fake scarcity, countdowns or unverified sustainability claims.
- Do not publish empty review modules.
- Persist selected variants when the customer opens guides or accordions.
- Track gallery use, size-guide opens, variant selection, Add to Cart and purchase errors.
