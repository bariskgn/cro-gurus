# Ryūma Stage 1: Homepage CRO Plan

**Goal:** Introduce Ryūma clearly, build trust and move visitors into the Launch Collection or a product page.

## Recommended section order

| Order | Section | What it contains |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Announcement bar | One verified message about launch, Swiss delivery or returns. Link to details. |
| 2 | Header | Logo, Shop, The Designs, Our Story and optional Journal; Search, Account and Cart. |
| 3 | Hero | Product-focused campaign image, clear value proposition and Shop the Collection CTA. |
| 4 | Reassurance strip | Up to three facts covering delivery, returns and product quality. |
| 5 | Launch Collection | Four to eight product cards or the complete small launch range. |
| 6 | Design stories | Two or three featured artworks, their meaning and links to products or full stories. |
| 7 | Quality proof | Material, fabric weight, construction and print/embroidery evidence with detail images. |
| 8 | Brand story | Short explanation of why Ryūma exists, its Japanese influence and design approach. |
| 9 | Shopping confidence | Links to Size Guide, Shipping, Returns and Contact. |
| 10 | Newsletter | Email signup for new releases, design stories and early access. |
| 11 | Footer | Shop, Help, About, Legal, social, payment and country/language links. |

## Hero content

Recommended working structure:

> **Japanese-inspired streetwear, made with purpose.**  
> Original designs, premium materials and the story behind every piece.

Primary CTA: **Shop the Collection**  
Secondary text link: **Explore the Designs**

Use a real garment on a person. The silhouette and artwork should be immediately visible. Prepare separate desktop and mobile crops and avoid placing text inside the image.

## Launch Collection

Product cards should contain:

- Primary product image
- Product name
- Price in the active currency
- Color swatches or color count
- Availability
- Truthful New or Limited badge

Use a two-column mobile and three/four-column desktop grid. Avoid a carousel and quick add at launch; direct customers to the PDP for fit and size information.

## Design stories

Each card should include:

- Artwork or garment detail
- Design name
- One-sentence meaning
- Discover the Story link

Keep cultural references accurate and specific. The design story should differentiate the product without becoming a long history lesson.

## Quality proof

Show three or four verified points:

- Material composition
- Fabric weight
- Fit or silhouette
- Construction details
- Print or embroidery method
- Production origin

Use close-up photography. Replace generic claims such as “premium quality” with specific product evidence.

## Brand and service content

The brand story should be one short paragraph with a founder, studio or lifestyle image. Link to the full Our Story page.

The shopping-confidence section should show:

- Find Your Size
- Shipping
- Returns & Exchanges
- Contact

Publish only confirmed delivery, duties and return terms.

## Visual direction

- Editorial, minimal and product-led
- Neutral background with one controlled accent
- Large lifestyle images balanced with product details
- Generous spacing without delaying products
- Minimal borders, icons and decorative effects
- Brand display type for headings; readable sans-serif for commerce content
- Japanese influence through art direction and storytelling, not clichés

Use subtle motion only where it clarifies interaction. Avoid autoplay video and rotating announcement bars.

## Mobile requirements

- Keep logo, Search and Cart visible.
- Show the hero CTA within the first screen where practical.
- Place products before long storytelling.
- Use a two-column product grid.
- Avoid essential swipe-only carousels.
- Use mobile-specific image crops.
- Keep tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
- Make country/language controls easy to find.

## Content and asset requirements

- Desktop and mobile hero images
- Product-card and alternate images for every product
- Two or three design-story images
- Three or four material/construction close-ups
- One founder, studio or brand image
- Confirmed product, shipping, returns and size information
- Final value proposition and newsletter promise

## CRO rules

- Visitors should understand what Ryūma sells within five seconds.
- Show products before long brand content.
- Use one primary action per section.
- Keep headings and supporting copy short.
- Do not publish empty reviews or invented testimonials.
- Avoid fake scarcity, countdowns and unsupported sustainability claims.
- Keep shipping, returns and sizing easy to find.
- Optimize and lazy-load below-fold images.
- Maintain accessible contrast, focus states, heading order and alt text.

## Tracking

Track:

- Hero CTA clicks
- Navigation clicks
- Product-card clicks
- Design-story clicks
- Size, shipping and returns clicks
- Newsletter signups
- Search use
- Homepage-to-PDP and homepage-to-collection rate
- Add-to-cart and purchase rate from homepage sessions

## Launch priorities

### P0

Header, hero, Launch Collection, verified reassurance, design stories, quality proof, shopping-confidence links, newsletter, footer and analytics.

### P1

Add verified reviews or customer imagery, then test hero copy, section order and product-grid presentation once traffic is sufficient.
