# Ryūma Stage 1: Shopify Theme and App Recommendations

**Goal:** Build a premium, flexible storefront with the smallest practical app stack.

**Reviewed:** 26 August 2026. Features and pricing should be rechecked before purchase.

## Theme recommendation

### Primary: Prestige

[Prestige by Maestrooo](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/prestige/styles/allure) is the strongest fit for Ryūma.

Why:

- Premium editorial presentation
- Strong high-resolution imagery and lookbook support
- Product tabs, size chart, swatches and image zoom
- Slide-out and sticky cart
- Product filtering, sorting and enhanced search
- EU-language translations
- Restrained styling that suits Ryūma better than a promotion-heavy layout

The official listing currently shows **$400 USD**. Test the theme with real Ryūma products, fonts and mobile images before purchasing.

### Alternative: Stiletto

[Stiletto by Fluorescent Design](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/stiletto/presets/stiletto) is the recommended alternative.

Strengths:

- Premium, minimalist styling for clothing and editorial content
- Flexible sections, presets, fonts and visual settings
- Developer-friendly structure for deeper customization
- Strong metafield support
- Sticky buy buttons, cart upsells and free-shipping bar
- Swatches, filters, enhanced search and recommended products
- German, French, Italian and Spanish theme translations

Stiletto offers more conversion features out of the box, while Prestige provides the calmer luxury foundation preferred for Ryūma. Avoid Stiletto's countdown and urgency features unless they support a genuine limited release. The official listing currently shows **$440 USD**.

## Theme selection checklist

Test both themes with:

- One T-shirt, hoodie and cap
- Long and short product names
- English and German content
- CHF and EUR
- Mobile hero and product images
- Cart drawer and unavailable variants
- Size-guide content
- Slow mobile connection

Choose the theme only after these flows work without custom apps or heavy code.

## Launch app stack

| Need | Recommendation | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Search, filters and recommendations | [Shopify Search & Discovery](https://apps.shopify.com/search-and-discovery) — free; supports filters, synonyms, boosts and recommendations | Launch |
| Translation and market adaptation | [Shopify Translate & Adapt](https://apps.shopify.com/translate-and-adapt) — free; use for German and market-specific copy | Launch if multilingual |
| Email capture and automation | [Klaviyo](https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing) — welcome, abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows | Launch if lifecycle email is ready |
| Product reviews | [Judge.me](https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme) — verified reviews, photo/video reviews and rating widgets | Install near launch; display after real reviews exist |

## Use native Shopify features first

No extra app should be required initially for:

- Cart drawer and sticky Add to Cart
- Size guide
- Product swatches
- Product recommendations
- Shopify Markets and currencies
- Shopify Payments, TWINT and accelerated checkout
- Customer accounts and guest checkout
- Basic self-service returns
- Metafields and design-story metaobjects

Build sizing, materials and design stories with theme blocks plus metafields/metaobjects. This keeps the store faster and easier to maintain.

## Upsell strategy

| Stage | Setup | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Launch: under 20 products | Use Shopify Search & Discovery with manually selected complementary and related products | Show one relevant recommendation; no upsell app |
| Growth: 20+ products | Evaluate Rebuy or a lighter alternative | Add only when catalog depth and order data can support useful offers |
| Optimization | Test placement, offer and product logic | Keep only offers that improve profit without reducing conversion |

[Shopify Search & Discovery](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-recommendations) can manage manual and automatic complementary/related recommendations. This is sufficient for Ryūma's first stage.

### Rebuy

[Rebuy Personalization Engine](https://apps.shopify.com/rebuy) is a strong choice when Ryūma needs:

- Personalized Smart Cart offers
- PDP, cart, checkout and post-purchase upsells
- Recommendation rules and AI
- A/B testing
- More advanced merchandising

Rebuy is powerful but likely excessive for a small launch catalog. Product count alone is not enough; Ryūma should also have meaningful order volume, several logical product combinations and a clear AOV opportunity.

### Lighter alternative: Selleasy

[Selleasy](https://apps.shopify.com/upsell-cross-sell-kit-1) is the better-value alternative when the requirement is limited to frequently bought together, PDP or cart offers. It is simpler and currently uses order-volume-based pricing.

**Recommendation:** Keep Rebuy as the preferred advanced option. Compare it with Selleasy after 20+ products. Choose Rebuy for personalization and full-funnel control; choose Selleasy for simpler manual offers and lower operating cost.

## Optional apps after launch

| Need | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Advanced returns platform | Return volume can no longer be managed efficiently in Shopify |
| Advanced search | Catalog size or search data exposes native limitations |
| Loyalty/referrals | Repeat purchase rate and customer demand justify it |
| Upsells/bundles | Catalog exceeds 20 products and Ryūma has clear product pairings, margin and order data |
| Back-in-stock | Products regularly sell out and are restocked |
| Customer service desk | Support volume exceeds shared-email management |

Do not install these “just in case.”

## App approval rules

Before adding any app, confirm:

- It solves a measured customer or operational problem.
- The theme or Shopify does not already provide the feature.
- Its scripts do not materially slow key pages.
- It supports all active languages and markets.
- Data access and GDPR/privacy requirements are acceptable.
- The team knows who owns configuration and support.
- Removal will not break theme content or customer data.

## Recommended setup

**Theme:** Prestige  
**Launch apps:** Search & Discovery, Translate & Adapt when German is live, Klaviyo if email flows are prepared, and Judge.me for review collection.  
**Upsells:** Native Shopify recommendations at launch; evaluate Rebuy against Selleasy after 20+ products.  
**Payment:** Shopify Payments with cards, accelerated checkout and TWINT for eligible CHF orders.

Keep the live app stack lean and review it quarterly.
