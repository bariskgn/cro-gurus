# Ryūma Stage 1: Collection Page CRO Plan

**Goal:** Make a small catalog feel curated, premium and easy to shop.

## Recommended section order

| Order | Section | What it contains |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Collection header | Collection name, one-line description and product count. Use one restrained campaign image only for editorial collections. |
| 2 | Category navigation | Visible chips: Shop All, T-Shirts, Hoodies & Sweatshirts and Launch Collection. |
| 3 | Controls | Product count and sort. Add filters only when the catalog has enough meaningful choices. |
| 4 | Product grid | Consistent product cards in a clean two-column mobile and three/four-column desktop grid. |
| 5 | Design-story block | Optional image, two lines about one featured design and a link to its story or product. Place after the first product row. |
| 6 | More products | Pagination or Load More. Preserve the visitor’s scroll position when returning from a PDP. |
| 7 | Reassurance strip | Short links to Size Guide, Shipping and Returns. |

## Product card content

- Clear primary product image
- Second image on desktop hover
- Product name
- Price in the active currency
- Color swatches or color count
- Availability
- Truthful badge such as New or Limited

The whole card should link to the PDP. Do not use quick add at launch; customers need size and fit context first.

## Filters and sorting

- **Under 12 products:** category chips only
- **12–30 products:** size, color, category and availability
- **30+ products:** add fit, price and audience when useful

Default sort: Featured. Also offer Newest and price sorting. Show active filters as removable chips and include Clear all.

## Visual direction

- Large, consistent imagery with generous spacing
- Minimal borders and interface chrome
- Neutral background with one brand accent
- Short editorial copy; products remain dominant
- No carousel for the main product range

## Important states

- **No filter results:** explain the issue and offer Clear filters
- **Sold out:** keep visible only when restock or waitlist is relevant
- **Small collection:** show all products without artificial pagination
- **Loading:** reserve image space to avoid layout movement

## Mobile requirements

- Keep category chips horizontally scrollable
- Use a sticky Sort/Filter control only after filters are activated
- Maintain readable names and prices
- Make swatches and controls easy to tap
- Avoid oversized collection banners that delay products

## CRO rules

- Products should appear within the first screen or immediately after the header.
- Do not publish empty collections or filters with one value.
- Use manual merchandising for the Launch Collection.
- Mix silhouettes and avoid near-identical cards in sequence.
- Show sold-out products after available products.
- Track collection views, product clicks, filter use and sort use.
