# Ryūma Stage 1: Customer, Competitor and Apparel-Market Research

**Date:** 25 August 2026  
**Scope:** Switzerland and Europe; men's T-shirts, hoodies and caps  
**Method:** Client brief, market studies and competitor desk research

> **Assumption to confirm:** “ZENKAI” is treated as [Zenkai Clothing](https://zenkaiclothing.com/), because its graphic-apparel positioning matches the brief.

## Executive summary

Ryūma has a credible position in accessible-premium European streetwear, but “premium” and “meaningful” are not unique claims. The opportunity is to combine a calm, recognizable brand with unusually clear product proof and Swiss-local convenience.

Recommended position:

> **Swiss-born, story-led everyday streetwear: original designs, visible product quality and a frictionless European buying experience.**

Key conclusions:

- Switzerland is attractive: NIQ-GfK estimates annual clothing-and-footwear purchasing power at **€1,096 per person**, 42% above the 25-country European average.
- Online demand is established: Eurostat reports **77% of EU people bought online in 2024**, and 70% of recent online shoppers bought clothing, footwear or accessories.
- Customers need value proof: inflation-adjusted EU clothing and footwear spending declined **0.6% in 2024**.
- Ryūma's CHF 69 T-shirt sits near Daily Paper's €64.95 level and must be supported by fabric, construction, fit, origin and design evidence.
- The CHF 79 hoodie is unusually close to the T-shirt price and should be checked for margin and premium-price consistency.
- Swiss shoppers expect CHF, TWINT, transparent delivery costs, guest checkout and simple returns.
- Zalando is the service benchmark; Zenkai, A-COLD-WALL*, Daily Paper and Nude Project are brand/story references.

## 1. Market findings

### Demand and spending

| Finding | Evidence | Ryūma implication |
|---|---|---|
| Online fashion is mainstream | [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/interactive-publications/digitalisation-2025): 70% of recent EU online shoppers bought apparel, footwear or accessories | Customers will compare Ryūma with mature stores |
| Swiss fashion spending is strong | [NIQ-GfK](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2025/europeans-spend-an-average-of-772-euros-on-fashion/): €1,096 per person | Switzerland is suitable for accessible-premium launch pricing |
| Direct brands can win | [Swiss Post/HWZ](https://digital-commerce.post.ch/-/media/post-maxisites/e-commerce/dokumente/E-Commerce-Stimmungsbarometer-2024-EN.pdf): 65% preferred buying from the manufacturer when given a choice | A credible DTC experience can compete with marketplaces |
| Value is under pressure | [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251120-2): real clothing/footwear spending fell 0.6% in 2024 | “Premium” needs concrete justification |

### Swiss e-commerce expectations

Swiss Post/HWZ surveyed 7,620 respondents. The sample skews older, so its younger-audience findings should be validated directly.

Important behaviors:

- 76% of respondents under 34 use smartphones often or always for online shopping; 60% of ages 35–54 do the same.
- Main cancellation reasons included no Swiss delivery (78%), hidden costs (78%), delivery fees (70%), missing payment methods (70%), security concerns (69%), forced registration (53%) and long delivery time (54%).
- TWINT was preferred by 81% of respondents under 34 and 66% of ages 35–54; cards were preferred by 72% and 76% respectively.
- 75% wanted transparency about product origin; 69% valued product sustainability information.
- 95% valued easy returns, 93% clear return terms and 82% quick refunds.
- 88% cited products not meeting size or quality expectations as a return reason.

Therefore Ryūma should provide:

- Mobile-first browsing.
- CHF/EUR and market-correct payment methods.
- Shipping cost, timing and duties/taxes before checkout.
- Guest checkout.
- Garment measurements, model data and fit guidance.
- Materials, GSM, construction, production origin and decoration method.
- Simple self-service returns with clear physical handoff.

## 2. Customer research

These are working segments, not verified Ryūma personas.

| Segment | Motivation | Main concerns | Required proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity-led streetwear buyer | Distinctive, wearable design with meaning | Originality and brand credibility | Design story, styling and real brand voice |
| Quality-first upgrader | Better everyday garments | Whether CHF 69 is justified | GSM, material, construction, origin and close-ups |
| Convenience-sensitive buyer | Correct fit and low-friction purchase | Size, delivery, payment and exchange risk | Measurements, CHF/TWINT, delivery estimate and returns |
| Early supporter | Join an emerging brand | Whether a real community exists | Founders, process, launch access and real customer content |
| Gift buyer | Meaningful premium gift | Size risk and arrival timing | Packaging, delivery date, exchanges, caps and gift cards |

### Customer decision sequence

The store must answer, in order:

1. Is this product and style for me?
2. What makes the design different?
3. Is the garment actually high quality?
4. Which size should I buy?
5. Is the price justified?
6. Can I trust the company?
7. What will delivery cost and when will it arrive?
8. What happens if it is wrong?

### Strongest drivers and barriers

| Drivers | Barriers |
|---|---|
| Original artwork and story | Unknown brand |
| Premium styling | Premium price without evidence |
| Product quality and longevity | Fit uncertainty |
| Swiss origin and convenience | Hidden shipping/duties |
| Easy payments and exchanges | Generic premium claims |
| Early community appeal | Small range presented as an empty store |

## 3. Competitor analysis

### Summary

| Brand | Role | Price reference | Key lesson | Ryūma opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zenkai Clothing | Graphic/story reference; US-focused | Tees from $31.99; hoodies from $59.99; caps $34.99 | Explain each graphic and show customer reactions | More premium product evidence and European relevance |
| A-COLD-WALL* | Aspirational brand-world reference | Tees roughly £50–£95 list; hoodie around £125 list | Specific identity plus exact product specifications | Clearer service and less discount dependence |
| Zalando Switzerland | E-commerce service benchmark | Broad marketplace range | Familiar discovery, payments, sizing and returns | Match clarity without marketplace complexity |
| Daily Paper | Direct European premium streetwear | New tees around €64.95; hoodie around €159.95 | Cultural identity, collections and community | Calmer presentation and stronger product proof |
| Nude Project | Direct youth/community streetwear | Tees around €49; hoodies €79–€129 | Belonging, founders and content cadence | More timeless, mature and Swiss-premium positioning |

Prices are snapshots from official sites and may change.

### Zenkai Clothing

[Zenkai Clothing](https://zenkaiclothing.com/) combines subtle nostalgic graphics, design stories, customer imagery, fit guidance and bundles.

**Use:** Concise “story behind the design,” plain fit language and social context.  
**Avoid:** Made-to-order/blank-store cues, repetitive stories and US-only service framing.

### A-COLD-WALL*

[A-COLD-WALL*](https://www.a-cold-wall.com/pages/brand) owns a specific British industrial world. Its product pages include fabric weight, construction, composition and garment measurements.

**Use:** Proprietary brand codes and exact product facts.  
**Avoid:** Overly conceptual first-level copy, heavy discount framing and DDU surprise costs.

### Zalando Switzerland

Zalando offers familiar filters, CHF, TWINT, invoice, size support and [30-day returns](https://www.zalando.ch/faq/how-can-i-return-my-order.html).

**Use:** Clear size, delivery, payment, returns and self-service account patterns.  
**Avoid:** Marketplace-scale navigation, excessive filtering and promotion-heavy browsing.

### Daily Paper

[Daily Paper](https://dailypaperclothing.com/pages/about-us) connects African heritage, seasonal collections, collaborations and membership. Product pages combine model/fit details, composition, shipping and returns.

**Use:** A repeatable story-to-product system and community with cultural substance.  
**Avoid:** Mature-brand navigation volume, frequent sale emphasis and unsustainable content cadence.

### Nude Project

[Nude Project](https://nude-project.com/pt-pt/pages/about-us-2) positions itself as a premium creative platform with founder-led content, frequent collections and community. It offers a duties-paid shipping option for Switzerland.

**Use:** Human founders, belonging and no-surprise Swiss delivery.  
**Avoid:** Overly Gen-Z tone, hype cadence and lifestyle content that obscures product detail.

## 4. Price and positioning

| Product | Ryūma | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| T-shirt | CHF 69 | Credible premium-streetwear price if quality is proven |
| Hoodie | CHF 79 | Accessible but only CHF 10 above the T-shirt; validate specification and margin |
| Cap | CHF 45 | Useful entry/gift product; still needs construction and fit proof |

Do not rely on launch discounts. If incentives are needed, use them for a defined purpose such as early access, first-order risk reduction or inventory management.

### Recommended position

> For design-conscious European customers who want clothing to carry meaning, Ryūma is a Swiss-born streetwear brand combining original visual stories, considered product quality and a polished buying experience.

Required reasons to believe:

- Original design authorship.
- Specific materials, GSM and construction.
- Clear fit and measurements.
- Real product and detail photography.
- Transparent delivery, duties and returns.
- Local payments and support.
- Verified reviews after launch.

## 5. CRO implications

### Launch-critical

- State what Ryūma sells and why it is different in plain language.
- Show products before long brand storytelling.
- Pair every design with a short meaning and deeper optional story.
- Put materials, fit, size, origin, care and decoration evidence on PDPs.
- Show CHF/TWINT and Swiss delivery clarity early.
- Keep guest checkout.
- Make returns/exchanges visible from PDP and cart.
- Present the small assortment as a curated Launch Collection.
- Do not publish empty Women, Best Sellers or Sale destinations.
- Do not fabricate reviews, scarcity or sustainability claims.

### After launch

- Collect verified product reviews and fit feedback.
- Add customer imagery with permission.
- Develop founder/designer content.
- Add related products and membership only when the catalog and customer benefit support them.

## 6. Validation plan

Before final design:

- Interview 8–12 target customers in Switzerland and priority EU markets.
- Run a five-second positioning test.
- Compare story-only product content with story + technical proof.
- Test whether customers can confidently select a size.
- Validate CHF 69/79 expectations using real samples.
- Test shipping, payment and return expectations by market.

## 7. Decisions required

1. Confirm the intended ZENKAI URL.
2. Select the primary launch audience within ages 16–45.
3. Rank the first European countries after Switzerland.
4. Confirm fabric, GSM, fit, decoration method and production country.
5. Confirm whether CHF 79 for the hoodie is final.
6. Define shipping thresholds, duties and return economics.
