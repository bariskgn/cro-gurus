Before / after wireframes

Shorten the distance from curiosity to connection.

Six wireframe comparisons show how to remove repeated choices, set expectations earlier, and focus the first session on one outcome: a safe, successful partner connection.

Primary KPI: partner connected / landing session Quality KPI: 30-second chat / landing session Guardrails: reports, blocks, underage signals

Preserve the visitor’s video or text intent through authentication and remove screens that do not help them connect.
